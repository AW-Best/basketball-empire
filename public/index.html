    BASKETBALL EMPIRE

    PLAYER RULEBOOK

    Build your franchise, outplay your rivals, and finish with the most PTS.

    2–4 PLAYERSHOST-SELECTED LENGTH18-SECOND TURNS
    01

    GET READY

    Create or join a room, choose your name and avatar, then press Ready. Every player begins on Tip-Off with 1,500 PTS. The host starts when at least two players are ready.

    02

    TAKE YOUR TURN

    Roll two dice and move clockwise one block at a time. Resolve the block where you stop, then end your turn. If both dice match, you roll again. The turn clock is 18 seconds.

    03

    SIGN ASSETS

    Land on an unsigned Team, Route, Offense Lab, or Defense Lab to buy it for the displayed price. If another player owns it, pay that owner the displayed landing payment.

    04

    5-SECOND AUCTION

    If the landing player passes on an available asset, every active player may bid. Use +2, +50, or +100. Every valid bid resets the clock to five seconds; the highest bidder wins and pays the bid.

    05

    BUILD A DIVISION

    Own every Team in one color division to recruit named Star Players. Develop teams evenly. More recruits create larger landing payments; a fully developed team becomes a Championship Lineup.

    06

    SPECIAL BLOCKS

    Team Ops and Game Time draw event cards. Routes earn more as you own more of them. Labs charge dice-based payments. Click any special block to read its exact rule.

    07

    TRADE

    Open the Trade Desk, choose an active rival, and offer PTS, undeveloped assets, or both. The receiving player must accept before anything moves. Mortgaged and developed assets cannot be traded.

    08

    TIP-OFF & BANKRUPTCY

    Passing Tip-Off earns 200 PTS; landing exactly on it earns 300 PTS. At 0 PTS, mortgage eligible undeveloped assets. If none remain, press Declare Bankruptcy; your assets return to the bank and you leave the match.

    09

    MOST PTS WINS

    The host chooses 10, 15, 20 minutes, or Unlimited before tip-off. When a timed match ends—or the host ends any match—the active player with the most available PTS is Champion. Purchased assets are not added to the final score.
