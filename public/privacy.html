Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

What this game processes

When you create or join a room, Basketball Empire processes the player name you enter, your game actions, room state, and a temporary access token used to reconnect you to that room. If you choose an uploaded avatar or camera photo, the image is shared with the players in that room and stored as part of the room state.

Browser storage

The game uses localStorage to remember your room session and preferences, including whether you dismissed the quick-start guide. You can remove this information by using Leave Game / New Game or clearing this site's browser data.

Google advertising

This site uses Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, IP addresses, and similar technologies to deliver, measure, and protect advertising. Depending on your location and consent choices, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, or limited.

Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services in Google's partner-sites policy. You can manage ad personalization through My Ad Center.

Service provider

The game is delivered through Cloudflare, which may process connection and security information needed to serve the website, protect it from abuse, and keep multiplayer rooms available.

Retention and choices

Room data is kept for game operation and may remain in service storage until removed during maintenance. Avoid uploading an avatar you do not want other room players to see. You may clear local browser data at any time and stop using the service.

Questions

Privacy questions may be submitted through the Basketball Empire project issue tracker.
