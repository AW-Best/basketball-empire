HOW TO PLAY

Pop. Charge. Dunk.

  1. Two phones. One match.Share a room code or invite link. Both players tap Ready to start. No download needed.
  2. Tap to shoot. Drag to aim.Match 3 of the same colour. Unsupported bubbles drop. Each normal bubble cleared gives 10 points and 16 energy.
  3. Hit 100 energy. Tap DUNK.Choose a move to clear your bottom row and send up to 4 junk bubbles. Teal means YOU attack; orange means your RIVAL attacks. A Dunk is an attack, not the end of the match.
  4. Break the junk bubbles.Cracked grey bubbles are junk. Match normal bubbles beside them to break them, or detach them so they drop. Junk gives no energy. During the warning, each group of 3 normal bubbles cleared cancels 1 incoming junk bubble. Up to 8 can be pending.
  5. Stay above the danger line.A new row arrives every 35 seconds. Touch the danger line and you lose; simultaneous contact is a draw. After a match, Play Again invites your rival. Both must agree to restart in the same room. DEMO01 restarts with one tap.

If a rival disconnects for 10 seconds, the match pauses and resumes when they return. Keep the game tab open for the best experience.
