# Sprint 003 — Tip-Off Landing Bonus

## User story

When a player's final movement step lands exactly on Tip-Off, that player receives 300 points.

## Acceptance criteria

- Exact landing on Tip-Off awards 300 points total.
- Passing Tip-Off without stopping there continues to award 200 points.
- Both outcomes record one completed lap.
- The player-facing Tip-Off guide explains both bonuses.
